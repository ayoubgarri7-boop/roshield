About

Made by a player, for players.

Who’s behind this site?

Hi! I’m a young Roblox developer. I created to protect my community: scams on Roblox and Discord mostly target young players, and the explanations out there are often complicated.

Here, everything is simple and direct. My goal is that you can spot a scam before you click.

What you’ll find here

  • The link checker: it checks a link without opening it and tells you if it comes from an official domain.
  • The scam guide: how scams work, how to spot them, and how to protect yourself.
  • The security checklist: the settings and habits that help protect your account.

Good to know

  • The link checker works only in your browser: the link you paste isn’t sent anywhere.
  • This site can help, but no tool protects you 100%. Stay alert and, if you’re ever unsure, talk to a trusted adult.
