Is this link official?

Paste the link someone sent you. This site checks it without ever opening it: Your link is checked on your device. For an “unknown” link, the full link is sent to our small service and then to VirusTotal, which may keep it and share it with the security community. Don’t paste a link here that contains anything private. Details: How it works and privacy. Your browser only downloads, like any other file on the site, a list of links that have already been reported, the same for everyone.

How it works and privacy

What should I do if I already clicked?

  1. Don’t type anything on the page: no password, no code you received, and don’t download anything. Close it.
  2. Change your password from the official app (not from a link), on a device you trust.
  3. Sign out of your other sessions (look in your security settings).
  4. Turn on 2-step verification if you haven’t already.
  5. If you opened or ran a file, tell an adult you trust: the device needs to be scanned with antivirus software.

How to read a link yourself

In https://www.roblox.com/games, the real site is roblox.com: the last two parts before the first “/”. In https://roblox.com.example.com/login, the real site is example.com, even though “roblox.com” is written at the start.

Some links that have already been reported are recognized thanks to the public list Phishing.Database (MIT license, license text). A list can be wrong.
